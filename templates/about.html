Project Overview

Agriculture plays an important role in food production and economic development. Selecting suitable crops and identifying plant diseases early can help farmers make informed decisions.

AgroVision is a web-based agricultural support system that combines Machine Learning and Deep Learning to provide crop recommendations and plant disease classification through a simple interface.

Users can enter soil and environmental values, upload leaf images, and view prediction results. Prediction history can also be stored in MySQL for administrative review.

WHAT AGROVISION OFFERS

Our Core Features

  • Soil pH and rainfall
  • Temperature, where supported

Plant Disease Detection

Analyze an uploaded leaf image to classify the plant and identify a possible disease.

  • Leaf image upload

  • Prediction history storage
🧠 CNN • MobileNetV2
🧠

Intelligence Behind AgroVision

The crop recommendation module uses a trained Random Forest classifier to learn patterns from agricultural data. The plant disease module uses a trained MobileNetV2 deep learning model to classify leaf images.

Each model processes its respective input and returns a prediction based on patterns learned during training. The quality of predictions depends on the training data and model performance.
